I am a Ph.D. student at the Golisano College of Computing and Information Sciences, Rochester Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Lingwei Chen. Before RIT, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from the APCS program at VNUHCM – University of Science, where I worked with Prof. Minh-Triet Tran.

I work on LLM post-training, self-improvement, and Trustworthy AI. I try to understand why a method works or fails, and then turn that into a simple, principled fix.
